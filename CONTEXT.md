# WE Bubbles: shared language

A glossary of the domain terms used in this project. Implementation details (tables, functions, libraries) do not belong here.

## Bubble

**Definition:** A learning group of 4 to 8 women around a shared professional topic. A Bubble owns its own syllabus (sessions and sections), its members, its waitlist and its resources, and has one Founder.
**Not to be confused with:** A "course". There is no separate course object. The syllabus lives inside the Bubble and is the Bubble's content.
**Resolved:** 2026-10-14, during the grill session on the backend plan.

## Fork

**Definition:** Opening a new Bubble from an existing Bubble's syllabus. The sessions and sections are copied into the new Bubble as an independent copy, and the new Bubble remembers which Bubble it came from. The woman who forks becomes the Founder of the new Bubble and can change her copy freely without affecting the original. Forking an existing syllabus is the encouraged path; building a syllabus from scratch is the secondary option.
**Not to be confused with:** Joining a Bubble (becoming a member of an existing group) and a suggested change (a proposal sent to a syllabus owner, not yet part of this model).
**Resolved:** 2026-10-14, during the grill session on the backend plan.
